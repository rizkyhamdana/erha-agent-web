Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how eR hA Agent ("we", "our", or "the app"), operated by creator Rizky Hamdana, collects, uses, and safeguards information when integrating with third-party platforms including TikTok via official TikTok Developer APIs.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize our application via TikTok OAuth Login, we may access the following minimal data solely to provide automated posting capabilities:

  • Basic Profile Information (Scope: user.info.basic): Your TikTok open_id, display name, and avatar URL to verify the destination account.
  • Content Posting Permissions (Scope: video.publish / video.upload): Temporary OAuth access tokens enabling the app to upload video files and associated captions directly to your authorized account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for the following functional purposes:

  • To authenticate your session as the authorized account creator.
  • To upload and publish rendered educational technology videos and captions created by you to your TikTok profile.
  • We do NOT sell, lease, or share your personal information or account credentials with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely in encrypted local environments or secured server configurations accessible only by the creator. We do not store your TikTok passwords or unapproved private profile details.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You may revoke this application's access to your TikTok account at any time through the TikTok App Settings > Security > Manage App Permissions. Upon access revocation, all cached session tokens associated with your account are immediately invalidated and purged.
